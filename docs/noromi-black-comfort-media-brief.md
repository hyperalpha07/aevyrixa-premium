## Shared production system

### Visual language

- Premium, calm feminine-care ecommerce photography; clean and product-focused rather than clinical.
- Use the finalized Noromi Care logo asset, preferably the horizontal logo when readable and the logo mark only when space is limited.
- Use the approved palette as supporting color, not as a filter that changes the garment:
  - Pink `#FF4F91`
  - Magenta `#E6008D`
  - Purple `#8A14C7`
  - Lavender `#E9D8FF`
  - Blush `#FFF0F6`
  - Gold accent `#D6A84B`
  - Deep purple `#3B0A59`
  - Soft background `#FCF7FD`
- The garment must remain a realistic neutral black with visible textile detail. Do not tint it purple, blue, or grey.
- Use genuine product photography or composites made from approved photographs of the exact SKU. Do not synthesize construction, stitching, packaging, accessories, or materials that are not present.
- Keep all important product details and text inside a central 80% safe area. The storefront uses a near-square contained main image and small square cropped thumbnails.

### Master and export settings

- Master canvas: 2000 x 2000 px, 1:1.
- Minimum acceptable delivery: 1600 x 1600 px, 1:1.
- Color: sRGB IEC61966-2.1.
- Working master: layered PSD, TIFF, or equivalent non-destructive source retained by the production team.
- Storefront delivery: WebP, high-quality photographic setting around 82–88, with visual inspection taking priority over an arbitrary compression number.
- Background: fully opaque; no accidental transparency or white fringe around the black garment.
- Suggested delivery target: under 600 KB when it can be reached without visible banding, text artifacts, or fabric-detail loss.
- Strip unnecessary metadata while preserving the correct color profile.
- Do not upscale a small source photograph to meet the master size.

Apply this negative prompt to all four assets:

> No Aevyrixa, Aevyrixa Her Care, Her Care, Hygeia, competitor branding, supplier watermark, marketplace badge, placeholder LOGO text, fake review, star rating, award, certification mark, OEKO-TEX, ISO, CE, FDA, medical symbol, laboratory iconography, shield implying antibacterial protection, Ag+, antibacterial, antimicrobial, germ-killing, named microorganism, infection claim, rash claim, irritation-prevention claim, odor-control claim, tampon comparison, pad comparison, 36ML, 40ML, any capacity number, all-night or overnight claim, 100% cotton, fabric percentage, unverified measurement, S–4XL range, 3XL, 4XL, absolute leak guarantee, “never leaks,” “100% leak-proof,” fake courier logo, delivery-time guarantee, free-delivery claim, or invented accessory.
