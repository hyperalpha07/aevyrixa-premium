Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right size?

Check our size guide based on your waist measurement. If between sizes, size up for more comfort. We stock XS to 3XL for all body types.

How long does delivery take?

Within Dhaka: 1–2 business days. Outside Dhaka: 3–5 business days. Express options available at checkout.

Is my packaging really discreet?

Yes, 100%. Plain unmarked package — no brand name or product description visible outside. Complete privacy, guaranteed.

Can I return or exchange my order?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days. Exchanges available for sizing issues. Used products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

How do I wash my period panty?

Rinse cold immediately after use. Machine wash cold on gentle cycle. No bleach or fabric softener. Air dry or tumble dry on low heat.
